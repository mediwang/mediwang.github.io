DawnBird is for people who sleep through alarms, lean on the snooze button, or switch the alarm off and drift right back. The sound starts soft and builds, so it never startles you. To turn it off, you solve a quick task first.

  1. 01

    A gentle start

    Every alarm sound begins quietly and builds to full volume. All 22 are original and melodic, in three intensities: gentle, bright and strong. No siren beeps.

  2. 02

    Solve to stop

    Choose math problems or a small Sudoku. Stopping from the lock screen or Apple Watch only quiets the alarm for a while; until the task is done, it rings again. The sound drops while you solve so you can think, and a wrong answer never resets your progress. Truly stuck? Hold Give Up for 3 seconds.

  3. 03

    Wake check

    A few minutes after you stop the alarm, DawnBird rings once more to check you didn’t fall back asleep. One tap on the lock screen confirms you’re up. You choose how many checks and how far apart.

  4. 04

    Holidays off, automatically

    Pick the U.S., Canada (by province) or Mexico, and DawnBird skips public holidays on its own, without asking the night before. Switch each holiday on or off, and add vacations as days off. The alarm card tells you ahead of time which day is skipped and why. One-time alarms always ring.

  5. 05

    See your progress

    A weekly report shows your average time to get up, your on-time streak and your fastest day, and you can share it as an image. The monthly report compares you with last month.

Quiet by design

  • No ads. No account.
  • Collects no data. Your alarms and records stay on your iPhone.
  • Asks for one permission only: alarms.
  • Built on the iOS system alarm, so it rings in Silent mode and during a Focus, without keeping the app open.

    Quelques minutes après l’arrêt de l’alarme, DawnBird sonne encore une fois pour vérifier que vous ne vous êtes pas rendormi. Un seul toucher sur l’écran verrouillé confirme que vous êtes debout. Vous choisissez le nombre de contrôles et leur intervalle.

    Suivez vos progrès

  • Une seule autorisation demandée : les alarmes.

DawnBird 是给叫不醒、爱按贪睡、关了闹钟又睡着的人做的。铃声从轻到响慢慢升起，不吓人；要关掉它，得先完成一个小任务。

  1. 01

    温柔开头

    每首闹铃都从轻声开始，逐渐升到满音量。22 首全部原创、有旋律，分温和、明快、强力三档，没有警报式的哔哔声。

  2. 02

    做题才算关

    可以选算数题或小数独。在锁屏或 Apple Watch 上按停，只会让它安静一会儿；题没做完，它会再响。做题时铃声会降低，让你能想清楚；答错不清零。真的做不出来，长按“放弃”3 秒即可停止。

  3. 03

    起床确认

    关掉闹钟几分钟后，DawnBird 会再响一次，确认你没有睡回去。在锁屏上按一下就算确认。次数和间隔由你决定。

  4. 04

    节假日自动跳过

    选美国、加拿大（按省）或墨西哥，DawnBird 会自己跳过法定节假日，前一晚不会来问。每个假日都可以单独开关，年假、出差可以设成休息日。跳过哪天、为什么跳过，闹钟卡片上会提前写明。一次性闹钟永远照常响。

  5. 05

    看见自己的进步

    周报显示平均起床用时、连续准时天数和最快的一天，可以生成图片分享。月报会和上个月对比。

干净、安静

  • 没有广告，没有账号。
  • 不收集任何数据，闹钟和记录只存在你的 iPhone 上。
  • 只申请闹钟这一项权限。
  • 基于 iOS 系统闹钟，静音模式和专注模式下照常响，不用一直开着 App。
